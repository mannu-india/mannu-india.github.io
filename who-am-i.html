Who Am I?

How does it matter if at all. As long as I can breathe and I am capable enough to think, I will grow.

Then wither away. I make mistakes and lose people, lose time, lose a part of myself and grow from those experiences.

I have one principle in life that I like to follow: Don't be afraid.

The worst will give you pain, the good will help you heal. Any action is better than inaction.

I find myself lucky to have parents and teachers who love me a lot. I really can't comprehend anything right now.
